# microgroove

The language of turning one audio file into a place and carrying a listener through it. Song-side terms name what is heard; place-side terms name what is seen.

## Language

### The song

**Section**:
A span of the song that analysis hears as one stretch of similar music.
_Avoid_: part, segment

**Onset**:
The instant a sound begins.
_Avoid_: hit, transient

**Pulse**:
The song's steady beat, present only where analysis trusts it.
_Avoid_: beat grid

**Intensity**:
How hard the song is pushing at one moment, as a single value that rises and falls over the song.
_Avoid_: energy, loudness

**Fingerprint**:
The measurements of the whole song that give its place an identity.
_Avoid_: profile

### The place

**Place**:
What a song becomes: the landscape and sky a listener is carried through, the same every time for the same audio.
_Avoid_: world, level, scene

**Region**:
The stretch of a place made from one section.
_Avoid_: zone, biome

**Route**:
The line through a place that the listener is carried along, laid out so that song time is distance.
_Avoid_: track, path

**Impulse**:
The place's visible answer to one onset.
_Avoid_: flash, pulse

**Moment**:
A place as it appears at one song time.
_Avoid_: frame

### Riding

**Listener**:
The person being carried through a place.
_Avoid_: player, viewer

**Journey**:
One audio file ridden end to end, hands off.

**Viewing**:
A launch the owner judges: one the owner is asked to judge, or one the owner starts unprompted and reports a verdict on that the work then acts on. Viewings are budgeted.

### Inspecting

**Still**:
A picture of one moment, made without a display.
_Avoid_: screenshot

**Timeline**:
A picture of what analysis heard, laid out over song time. It is for inspection and is never part of a journey.
_Avoid_: debug view
